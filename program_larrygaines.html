Getting Started
How This Works
01Your Training Program
TRAIN is an intelligent training platform built around progressive overload and real performance data. Your program runs in 8-week blocks, with every week designed to build on the one before it. Each session tells you exactly what to do — exercises, sets, reps, tempo, rest and effort (RPE and RIR) — with step-by-step form cues (Setup, Move and Key point) and voice-over demo videos on most exercises, with more added all the time.

As you log, TRAIN does the remembering. Last week's weight and reps sit right next to every set, a built-in rest timer keeps you on pace, and new personal records are flagged the moment you hit them. Quick check-ins before and after each workout track your sleep, energy, stress and soreness — and if something doesn't feel right, tap Pain Felt for safe, in-the-moment guidance.

Everything you log is backed up and goes straight to your coach, so your program is adjusted based on what's actually happening, not guesswork. Week 1 sets your baseline. At the halfway point, your Week 4 checkpoint shows what's working so far and asks three quick questions about pace, energy and what you'd change — a chance to adjust before the second half of the block. By Week 8, your report card shows how far you've come — strength, power, recovery and consistency — and maps out where your next block should start.
02Your Nutrition Guide
What you eat plays a major role in how you feel, perform, recover, and see results. The goal isn't a meal plan you follow forever — it's giving you the knowledge and tools to take charge of your own nutrition.

TRAIN gives you personalized calorie, protein, carbohydrate and fat targets, with meal ideas built from foods you actually enjoy, plus the reasons each one matters. Real life includes restaurants, travel, busy days and the occasional treat. Your targets give you direction. Your food choices are yours.
03MyFitnessPal — Food Tracking
For these 8 weeks, we track — not forever, but because data teaches us things guessing can't. Tracking shows how much energy you're actually eating, how your macros are split, and how that lines up with your training and recovery.

Use the free version of MyFitnessPal:
  • Go to Goal Settings and set a custom calorie and macro goal using the numbers in your Nutrition Guide.
  • Log meals as accurately and consistently as you can. Consistency beats perfection. Protein is the number I watch most.
  • In Settings → Sharing & Privacy → Diary Sharing, set your diary to Friends Only and send a request to so I can review your food log.
04Progress Photos
Your weight, measurements and lifts tell the story in numbers. Photos capture what they can't: shape, definition, posture and proportion. Upload once a week — front, back and both sides — in the same lighting, clothing and poses. You don't need to love the photo. You just need to take it.

The Partnership
What To Expect

Coaching works best as a two-way street. Here's what you can count on from me, and what I'll be counting on from you.

From Me

TRAIN handles the structure, progression and tracking. I coach you through the process.

  • I review your weekly check-in, progress photos, training data and food log to see the bigger picture
  • I adjust your training or nutrition when your data says it's needed
  • I'm available for questions, exercise swaps and form checks between sessions
  • You'll get honest, direct feedback — what you need to hear to keep moving forward
  • My goal is to teach you enough that you become confident and in control of your own progress

From You

Your job is to show up, collect the data, and communicate.

  • Log every workout — weight, reps, and how it felt
  • Upload your weekly progress photos by Sunday — front, back and both sides
  • Track your food in MyFitnessPal — protein first
  • Tell me when something's off: pain, poor sleep, stress, travel. Early beats late
  • Ask questions. Understanding why is part of the process
  • Be honest with your data. Accurate beats perfect
Optional, Not Required
Recommended Supplements & Tools

How to use your targets

These numbers are your daily targets, not a strict meal plan. Your protein target is the floor, not the ceiling. Structure meals around how you actually eat day to day.

Protein

Repairs and builds the muscle you break down in training — in a deficit, too little means your body pulls from muscle. It's also the most filling macro.

Carbs

Your primary fuel for training and recovery — they refill glycogen so sessions don't feel flat, and support brain function and mood.

Fat

Drives hormone production, supports brain health, and helps you absorb fat-soluble vitamins.
