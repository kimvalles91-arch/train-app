Your estimated maintenance is ~2,584 calories/day — that's roughly what your body burns in a day at your current size, age, and activity level, the number where weight stays the same. That's exactly where we're starting you, at maintenance, not a surplus yet. Muscle can still be built at maintenance, especially with your training experience and consistent protein intake — it's just a slower, cleaner build with less fat gain along the way than jumping straight into a surplus.

We're holding you at maintenance for now rather than jumping into a surplus. This gives us a clean window to dial in your training consistency, protein intake, and tracking habits before we deliberately start adding calories. Once that's locked in and you're recovering well between your 3 sessions a week, we'll move you into the lean bulk row above — a small, controlled surplus rather than the aggressive one.
These numbers are your daily targets, not a strict meal plan. Your protein target is the floor, not the ceiling. With only 2 main meals most days, that protein number is going to feel like a lot per sitting — snacks and a shake are there to help close the gap, not just to fill calories.

Protein

The direct building block for muscle tissue and the macro that matters most right now — even at maintenance, hitting this number consistently is what lets your training actually translate into muscle.

Carbs

Your primary fuel for training output and recovery — they replenish glycogen so your 3 lifting sessions a week stay strong and you can actually push in the gym.

Fat

Drives testosterone and hormone production — genuinely important for a muscle-building goal at any age — and supports joint and brain health.

Steak & Eggs Bowl

~1,050 kcal · 68P / 108C / 30F
  • 6 oz 93% lean ground beef, seasoned and pan-seared
  • 3 whole eggs + 3 egg whites, scrambled
  • 1.25 cups cooked white rice (or 1.5 medium potatoes, roasted)
  • 1 cup sautéed spinach or bell peppers, drizzle of olive oil
Swap: chicken thigh or lean beef for the ground beef · sweet potato for the rice

Eat-Out Order: Bowl Chain

~1,000 kcal · 62P / 115C / 27F
  • Regular portion chicken or steak (Chipotle-style)
  • White rice + black beans
  • Fajita veggies (peppers/onions)
  • Light scoop of guac or cheese
Swap: shrimp for the protein when it's on the menu · use this version on eat-out days — it's built to hit similar numbers to the home version
Note: since you eat out often, this is your go-to template — protein + rice + beans + veggies, hold the extra sauce/dressing.

Salmon & Sweet Potato

~1,050 kcal · 63P / 105C / 36F
  • 8 oz salmon, baked or pan-seared
  • 1.25 cups roasted sweet potato
  • 1 cup broccoli or asparagus
  • Drizzle of olive oil or a few slices of avocado
Swap: white fish (cod/tilapia) or tuna for the salmon · quinoa for the sweet potato

Eat-Out: Sushi Night

~950 kcal · 52P / 120C / 22F
  • Sashimi/nigiri combo — salmon, tuna, shrimp
  • 1 specialty roll (rice-based, not fried)
  • Edamame as a starter
  • Miso soup, optional
Swap: add an extra roll or sashimi order if you're short on calories that day
Note: since sushi's a favorite, easy on soy sauce (sodium) and skip the fried/tempura rolls to keep this in your macro range.

Well-Rounded Protein Smoothie

~400 kcal · 30P / 42C / 11F
  • 1 scoop whey protein
  • 1 banana
  • 1/3 cup oats
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 cup milk or water + a handful of ice, blended
Swap: plant protein powder for whey · almond butter for peanut butter · frozen berries for the banana if you want it lower-sugar
This one's built to be a real mini-meal, not just a protein hit — the oats and banana give you carbs to refuel with, the peanut butter adds fat and keeps it filling longer.

Protein-Forward

~250–350 kcal · 25–35P

  • 1 cup Greek yogurt + handful of berries
  • 1 cup cottage cheese + pineapple
With only 2 main meals, these snacks are doing real work to close your protein and calorie gap — don't skip them thinking they're "extra."
