Mobility modifications applied throughout all 8 weeks Tight hips — 90/90 hip stretch added to every lower body warm-up; wider stance and forward lean cued on all squat patterns; hip flexor priority in cooldowns. Hamstring tightness — RDL range limited initially and built progressively; 3-sec eccentric on all hamstring curl work; supine hamstring stretch in every lower body cooldown. Wrist discomfort — wrist mobility sequence in every upper body warm-up; neutral grip cued on all pressing; barbell pressing avoided where dumbbell or cable provides equal stimulus.
A note from TRAIN Kimberly — you built the system, now let it work for you. This program mirrors the structure you already know produces results: two glute-dominant lower days, two upper days split between strength and hypertrophy, active recovery in between. Every set, every cue, every mobility modification was built around your body, your history, and what three years of consistent training has taught you about yourself. This is not a program to figure out — it's a program to execute. Show up. Track your weights. Beat last week somewhere, every session. That's the whole game. Mesocycle 1 — Week 1
Foundation
Week one isn't about going hard — it's about going smart. Learn the TRISET format on Monday, get your baseline loads dialed in on every session, and establish the mind-muscle connection before we add intensity. You already know how to train. This week is about learning this program.
Mesocycle 1 — Week 2
Accumulation
You should be feeling it now. That's your body adapting. Push to the top of your rep ranges this week — if you hit the ceiling with 2+ reps in reserve, add load. Track every weight. These become your Week 3 targets.
Mesocycle 1 — Week 3
Intensification
This is the hardest week of the mesocycle. Primary movements go to 5 sets. RPE 8–9 across the board. You've been building to this — dig deep and finish strong. Week 4 is earned by what you do here.
Mesocycle 1 — Week 4
Deload
I know this week feels light. It's supposed to. The adaptation happens in recovery — not in the gym. You're going to want to go harder. Don't. Trust the process. Week 5 is going to feel like a different body.
Recovery week
Same movements. 2 sets. 60% of Week 3 loads. No conditioning finishers on Friday. Supercompensation happens here — let it.
Mesocycle 2 — Week 5
New Baseline
Feel that? That's what we've been building. Your starting point today is above where you were five weeks ago. New exercise variations introduced this week — the hip thrust becomes a QUAD-SET, the incline press moves to dumbbells, the RDL gets heavier. Build from here.
Mesocycle 2 — Week 6
Accumulation
Beat your Week 5 loads. Primary movements add a set this week. The compound effect of consistency is starting to be undeniable — in your numbers, in your body, in how you carry yourself.
Mesocycle 2 — Week 7
Peak
Your strongest week yet. RPE 8–9–10 on all primaries. Maximum sets. Every load you've added over the last 6 weeks has been building toward this session. You've earned every rep. Go get it.
Mesocycle 2 — Week 8
Final Deload & Reflection
Eight weeks. You showed up every time. Reflect on your hip thrust load in Week 1 versus today. Compare your pull-up assistance, your RDL depth, your lateral raise weight. That gap between then and now — that's yours. Rest this week. Then let's build the next block.
Program complete
2 sets. 60% loads. Same movements, same discipline. Recover fully. You've already begun the next version of yourself.
